## Current scope

- Preserve guest Practice/study, 15-second timer, feedback, local results and export. Login unlocks separate Ranked/Profile/leaderboard/Support flows.
- Normal Ranked: solo, 15 questions, five per difficulty, ten seconds each, server-owned assignments/deadlines and once-only settlement; immediate answer feedback. Ranked/promotion feedback stays visible until Next; only then does the next question’s ten-second timer begin. Final-answer completion still settles under existing rules; season/connection limits and tie-break secrecy remain unchanged.
- Easy +10/-15, Medium +20/-10, Hard +30/-5; timeout wrong. Raw regular range -150 through +300. Negative totals allowed.
- One balance, overflow discarded, perfect promotions and mandatory re-promotion after demotion. Correct promotion answers add zero; mistakes/timeouts deduct normally.
- Q7 entry settled: Promotion entry uses an explicit “Start promotion” action. Show that a perfect 15/15 quiz is required before the player starts. Reaching the current badge cap makes promotion available but does not automatically create or start a promotion attempt; merely opening the promotion information does not start the quiz or its question timer. The player chooses when to initiate the attempt. Existing promotion scoring, cap+1 success, demotion/re-promotion, one-active-attempt and season-admission rules remain unchanged. At a capped badge’s settled point cap (Iron 1,000, Bronze 2,000, Silver 3,000, Gold 4,000), Start promotion is the only Ranked entry option; ordinary Ranked quizzes cannot start. Practice remains available unchanged. Below that badge’s cap, ordinary Ranked quizzes remain available under existing season/active-attempt rules. Challenger is uncapped and has no promotion gate. Apply this gate when starting a new attempt using current server state; do not interrupt an existing quiz or cap individual answers. A failed promotion that settles below the cap returns the player to ordinary Ranked until eligible again.

- One active attempt per account, including original-session 15-second grace. Current timer continues; no unseen next question while disconnected. Grace expiry settles partial score; Quit applies a -10 adjustment before the rank cap (Q1 settled); apply the cap once to the final attempt result, never per answer (Q2 settled). Terminal attempts never resume.
- Q8 refresh policy settled: Refreshing or reloading the original quiz tab must not allow the attempt to be resumed, even within the 15-second grace period. Grace-based continuation is limited to recovery in the original still-running quiz session. This applies to ordinary Ranked, promotion and prize tie-break attempts. Another tab/device still cannot resume or replace the attempt. No page reload may reset a question clock, erase accepted answers, or bypass the one-active-attempt rule. After refresh-related connection loss, finalize the attempt at the existing server-controlled 15-second connection-loss deadline. The refreshed page cannot rejoin during that window. Reloads, repeated reconnect requests or new tabs do not reset or extend the deadline or question clock. Keep the account’s active-attempt lock until the server finalizes exactly once. Ordinary Ranked/promotion settle accepted partial results under their existing rules, without an extra Quit penalty; prize tie-breaks become incomplete with no same-round retry or seasonal-point adjustment. Current-question timeout and no-unseen-question rules, confirmed-outage exceptions and season-closing rules remain unchanged. Q8 remains open for refresh/session enforcement, server-confirmed loss/outage signals and grace-start/cadence details; do not claim every refresh is instantly detected. Partial-score settlement and explicit-Quit-only penalties retain the existing rules; a refresh is not automatically assigned the extra -10 Quit penalty. An account’s active attempt remains active until the server finalizes it under the approved termination policy. Confirmed-outage exceptions remain unchanged.
- Confirmed server-wide outage voids affected normal attempts. Tie-break outage policy instead restarts the current round for all participants. No reliable intent/hardware-device detection claim.
- Case-insensitive unique usernames, permanent unique six-character uppercase letter/number tags, optional private email, no required real name/employee ID or phone collection. Verified private email supports self-service password-reset links to the same account, separately from reminder consent; recovery without verified email and protocol/provider details remain open Q10/Q11.
- Non-punitive server connection flags, player-requested private Support → Tickets, 15-day closed descriptions/unshared-event retention, open-case/score-history preservation. Shared detailed connection logs stay while any linked ticket/admin review is open, then are deleted 15 days after the last linked case closes; case-specific descriptions keep their own cleanup dates (Q9 settled). Routine admins cannot change points.

## Seasons, prizes and display

Owner launches from Pre-Season on a date still undecided. Reset current points/rank/promotion to 0/Iron/none while retaining accounts/history. Official close: December 31, 2026 at 11:59 p.m., America/New_York. Existing attempts get five extra minutes, then partial settlement/freeze. Q13 settled: unfinished Pre-Season Ranked/promotion attempts, including grace/feedback states, end without applying scores or promotions before reset. Preserve completed and launch-ended history in the owner-private profile tab “pre season history”, with server-side ownership enforcement and no other-player access or automatic admin exception; no Pre-Season results may enter official points/prize qualification, including late/racing submissions. Practice and private Support data stay unchanged. No January start is approved.

Three winners receive one UPS hat each. Q5 eligibility is settled: owner-approved sorters in Primary Sort on Twilight at Saddle Brook only, one fixed prize account/hat per person, owner approval before scheduled season close, and one valid completed Ranked quiz credited to the official season. Already-approved players may complete that quiz within the finishing window; negative completed scores count. Pre-Season/void/unfinished/tie-break-only histories do not qualify. Recovery keeps the same account, with no point transfer. Public identity remains username/tag.

The official competition leaderboard includes only eligible players. Ineligible accounts may still play and view their own points/badge, but have no competition position; they are not automatically deleted or blocked. Eligibility does not change points or partial-score rules.

Prize tie-breaks auto-advance after server-accepted answers/timeouts without a Next button or feedback pause, with one active question and its round-specific timer at a time. After question five show submission confirmation only; no next question while disconnected. Ordinary Ranked/promotion still waits for Next. Prize tie-breaks use five fresh shared Hard sort-work-methods questions, 0–5 score, once per participant/round, without seasonal-point changes. The first 24-hour window starts at scheduled season closing; later rounds get fresh 24-hour windows. Challenger: maximum three valid rounds at five seconds/question. Lower ranks: maximum five valid rounds at 5/5/5/4/3 seconds/question. Outage restarts retain the current timer and do not consume a valid round; the entire interrupted round restarts for all participants with fresh questions/deadline. Remaining ties after the maximum or unavailable fresh approved questions go to owner selection. Lower-rank unresolved incomplete/no-show places after the deadline go to owner selection below completed attempts. At the close of a valid Challenger tie-break round’s 24-hour window, automatically exclude participants whose attempt is incomplete or who did not participate from that season’s top-three prize positions. If both players fail to complete, both are excluded. Keep their frozen seasonal points, badge and history unchanged; this affects prize placement only. A completed 0/5 attempt remains completed. A confirmed server outage voids/restarts the whole round under the existing policy, so a voided round or its superseded deadline cannot trigger this exclusion. Q14 backfill approved: After automatic Challenger noncompletion/no-show exclusion, fill vacant top-three prize places from the next eligible, non-excluded players using frozen seasonal points and the existing prize tie-break rules. Do not change points or badges, reinstate excluded players, or reopen already resolved prize positions. A tie newly affecting a prize must be resolved before a winner is finalized; do not invent ordering between tied candidates. A newly affected replacement tie group gets a fresh 24-hour window measured from the opening of its round, not the original season-close deadline or another group’s deadline. All players in that round share its deadline; per-question timers, feedback release, one-attempt rule, exclusion handling and confirmed-outage restarts retain the existing tier-specific policies. Preserve frozen seasonal points and already resolved prize places. If fewer than three eligible, non-excluded players remain after the approved backfill/tie-break process, award one hat to each available confirmed winner and leave every remaining hat unawarded. If nobody remains eligible, award no hats. Do not reinstate excluded players, waive eligibility, invent winners, change frozen points/badges, or reopen resolved placements to force three awards. Unresolved ties or an owner selection still pending are not candidate exhaustion; finalize those cases under the existing rules first. No first-to-reach timestamp fallback remains (Q6 settled). Keep one attempt, original-session 15-second grace, continuing current question clock/no unseen next question, Quit or grace expiry as incomplete/no retry and no seasonal deduction. Hide scores/correctness/answers/review until the current round deadline; normal Ranked feedback stays immediate. See [[2026-11-07-ranked-decision-record]] for the controlling details.

Leaderboard requires login and updates after settlement; provisional until final resolution. Ranked landing page hosts season notices/countdowns. Keep main-home sort-change announcements. One-time verified-email launch reminders require separate consent, cancellation and at most three send attempts within the first hour; provider details remain open.
