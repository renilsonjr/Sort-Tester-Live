What this would mean in practice. One breakpoint at 900px: below it, nothing changes at all — the phone keeps its bottom dock and single column. Above it, the dock moves to the side and three screens gain a second column (Home, Leaderboard, Profile). The quiz, the chart and the forms stay narrow and centred.

The keyboard. Desktop means a keyboard, so the quiz gets number keys 1–4 for the belts and Esc for Cancel, with a visible focus ring everywhere. That is the third box on the issue and it is drawn here on purpose.

Not drawn yet: Practice, the chart, promotion, Profile and Owner tools. They follow the same two rules — forms and quizzes narrow, lists and dashboards two columns — and I would draw them in the PR that implements each.
