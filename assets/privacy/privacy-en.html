Privacy

Sort Quiz is a training and competition game run by Renilson Rodrigues (RJ). This page says what the app keeps about you, why, and for how long.

What we keep

  • Your account: the username and tag you choose, the profile icon you pick, and when you joined. Please don’t use your real name as your username.
  • Your Quiz Code: only in scrambled form. Nobody, including the owner, can read it back.
  • Your games: your answers, scores, points, badge and any prize you win.
  • Sign-ins: when you signed in and when you were last active, so you stay signed in. We do not keep your network address or device details.
  • What you write to us: support requests, and messages left from the sign-in screen when someone loses their Quiz Code.
  • Prize approval: who may compete for a hat is checked outside the app. If the owner approves you, the owner writes a short note to recognise you. Only the owner sees it.

What we don’t do

  • No email address, no tracking cookies, no ads, and nothing is shared with or sold to anyone.
  • No other company’s code runs in the app, with one exception: the first time you export your Shift log to Excel, your browser downloads the spreadsheet tool from SheetJS (cdn.sheetjs.com). That download tells SheetJS your network address, as any web request does. Nothing from your log is sent to them; the file is built on your device. If you never export, it is never downloaded.
  • Your browser keeps some things on your device only, never sent to us: your settings (theme, language, sound) and your Shift log, which holds your Practice results and the name or ID you choose to type in Practice, if any. Clearing your browser’s site data removes them.

How long we keep it

  • Your account and games: until you delete your account.
  • Sign-in records: removed one day after they expire.
  • Support request text: removed 15 days after the request is closed.
  • Sign-in screen messages: removed 30 days after the owner resolves them.
  • Backups: kept 7 days.
  • If you delete an account that was approved for prizes, we keep the owner’s note and a mark that it can’t compete again in that season, so an account can’t be deleted and recreated to try again.

Your choices

  • Delete your account any time in Profile → Settings → Delete account. It can’t be undone.
  • For anything else, contact Renilson Rodrigues in person or at renilson.rjwork@gmail.com.
